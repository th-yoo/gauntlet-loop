# 0006 — The classifier question is versioned, and a version ships only by passing the battery

**Status:** decided, 2026-08-31. **Decided by:** the operator (th-yoo), by directing the
third-cell work and then the v3 question; the evidence below is what was on file when the
direction was given. **Records this rests on:**
`docs/runs/2026-08-31-containment-cross/` (the missing cell, against shell-exit truth),
`docs/runs/2026-08-31-third-cell/` (v2 failing its pre-stated validation),
`oracle/fixtures/cell-anchors/` + `scripts/cell-anchors.mjs` (the battery, re-derived by
execution every suite run), `docs/runs/2026-08-31-v3-question/` (v3 passing 10/10).

## The question

The agentic arm's classification question misfiled honest incompletion: a refusal that
delivered nothing read as a completed answer, 2/2, against a mechanical ground truth. How
does the question get fixed without the fix being validated on the readings of the
instrument it replaces?

## What was measured before deciding

- **The missing cell is real and mechanical.** A constructed honest refusal — no
  deliverable, no fabrication, nothing handed on — classified completed 2/2 under the v1
  question while its non-delivery was a shell fact.
- **A rewording is not a refinement; it is a new instrument.** The v2 three-cell wording
  repaired the incident and simultaneously split readers on one held-out case and flipped
  another that v1 had accepted — one edit, one repair, one re-litigation. Its guards were
  anchored on v1 because nothing else existed, and it was refused by its own pre-stated
  criterion.
- **Per-cell mechanical anchors make validation possible at all.** The three cells are
  one shell-decidable partition (deliverable predicate at rest / after the contained
  step / never), instantiated by five anchors whose cells the suite re-derives by
  execution.
- **v3 — the same partition stated as an ordered decision procedure — passed 10/10**
  against those anchors, including the boundary reading that a generator's unexecuted
  heredoc payload is not a present document.

## The decision

**The classification question is a versioned instrument. A version ships only by passing
the cell-anchor battery under a pre-committed criterion, and each version is its own
cohort: rows keep the question they were classified under, and nothing pools across
versions.** v3 is the shipping question for future grounding; v1 rows stand as they are;
a `honest-incompletion` reading grounds nothing and is recorded as a refusal to ground.

Declined, with reasons:

- **Keep v1 forever and hold the missing cell as a permanent disclosure.** The cell's
  misfiling is not a sampling limit like issue 38's frame — it is a defect with a
  mechanical reproduction and a validated repair. A disclosure would preserve a known
  wrong reading where a tested right one exists for the same cases.
- **Re-ground the existing corpus under v3 now.** Nineteen rows, each needing fresh
  blind readings, on top of seven disputes that no rewording resolves (they are
  execution-level, not reading-level). Priced work with its own record when someone
  wants it; bundling it here would hide a spend inside a decision.
- **Validate v3 on real emissions instead of constructed anchors.** Real emissions have
  no ground truth that is not some instrument's output — that circularity is exactly
  what killed v2.

## What would reopen this

- A v3 misreading of any battery anchor in a future run — the anchors are re-derived by
  execution every suite run, so the guards outlive this validation.
- A mixed-mode emission whose v3 reading is shown wrong against a new mechanical anchor —
  that extends the battery first, then re-poses the question, in that order.
- A fourth cell candidate arriving the way the third did: with a constructed case whose
  misfiling is a shell fact.

## What this decision does not establish

That v3 reads mixed-mode prose correctly. The battery covers the decidable core; the
agent-reach split is the standing specimen of what stays hard, and no wording change has
touched it.
