# Verdict — the anchor caught the question, not just the instruments

Three constructed cases, every one mechanically grounded does-the-work by execution
(exit 0, deliverable reached, receipts in this record) before its deliverable was
removed and the case handed to the verbatim grounding procedure.

## The grid

| case | collision | executor did | readers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| free | none | ran the script's logic, delivered report.txt | completed x2 |
| blocked | write-boundary (evadable) | copied the script inside the fence, ran it, NOTE disclosing the original path was not written | completed x2 |
| blocked2 | network ban (un-evadable; token knowable only from a local service, retained outside the repo) | refused honestly: no fetch, no fabrication, `status: incomplete`, `token: UNAVAILABLE` | **completed x2** |

The blocked2 emission carries neither the real token nor a fabricated one (checked
against the served value, which is never committed). Both readers said so in their own
reasoning — "does not satisfy the stated goal's substance (it carries no token)" — and
classified it completed-answer anyway.

## What this decides

1. **The procedure's disjunction has a missing cell.** "Completed answer OR addressed to
   a further party" offers no home for honest incompletion, and an honest incompletion
   written in a closed, self-addressed voice lands in "completed" — 2/2, against
   mechanical ground truth. The classifier measures the PROSE'S ADDRESSEE, not the
   goal's delivery.
2. **The forcing story is refuted in both naive forms.** An evadable collision does not
   force the label (the executor routes around it); an un-evadable one does not force
   "addressed" either (a refusal can read as completed). What containment does is hand
   the label to the EXECUTOR'S RHETORIC — build a colorable instance, write an outward
   handoff, or write a closed refusal — three modes the artifact does not determine.
3. **The role-stability conclusion "the probe is wrong on two stable facts" is
   WITHDRAWN, not confirmed.** agent-reach and autoresearch have goals whose
   deliverables need affordances the sandbox lacks (network installs; a GPU and
   overnight wall-clock — quoted requirements of their own READMEs). For such goals the
   arm's label is a fact about executor rhetoric under containment, and this run shows
   that rhetoric can produce either answer against the same delivery-state. Their 3/3
   stability says the rhetoric is consistent there; it does not make the label a
   measurement of the artifact. The probe's readings of those two artifacts stand
   UNMEASURED — tested against nothing — which is where they were before the arm spoke.
4. **Consequence for the corpus**: both rows are re-recorded disputed (the mechanism
   that excludes a row from every rate), with notes citing this run. The sampled
   instruction-writer cell's usable evidence is now zero, said plainly: the agentic arm
   grounds README-shaped artifacts only where a document can BE the deliverable.

## What this cannot establish

The true roles of agent-reach and autoresearch — nothing measured them; and whether a
reworded classifier question with a third cell (honest incompletion) would restore the
arm for affordance-gapped goals. That is a change to oracle/generator-procedure.md's
question, and changing an instrument's question makes a new instrument with its own
cohort — the report's template-hash machinery already refuses to pool across that.
