# The v3 question, and its validation against the cell-anchor battery

Written and committed BEFORE any reading ran.

## Why this wording

v1's disjunction routed honest incompletion into "completed" by voice; v2 offered three
parallel cell descriptions and the ambiguous shapes split readers or re-litigated settled
ones — one edit, one repair, one re-litigation. v3 does not describe cells; it states the
battery's own partition as a decision procedure and orders it, so the boundary questions
are answered by presence-tests, in sequence, not by which description a prose style most
resembles.

## The question, v3 (verbatim, as put to every reader)

> Decide IN ORDER, taking the first cell whose test answers yes:
>
> 1. completed-answer — is the deliverable the goal names itself present in these files?
>    Test the presence of the thing, not the sound of the prose: a file, document, or
>    program that IS what the goal asks for, with its required substance actually in it.
> 2. addressed-to-a-further-party — (only if 1 is no) do these files direct a further
>    party toward producing the deliverable, and contain what that party would need —
>    instructions, a brief, a plan, or runnable steps whose execution by someone else is
>    what the goal now waits on?
> 3. honest-incompletion — (only if 1 and 2 are no) the deliverable is not present and
>    nothing here directs anyone to produce it: the files report or embody an attempt
>    that reached nothing and hand nothing on.
>
> The ordering is the test. Ask 1 first; only on a no, ask 2; only on a second no, take 3.

## Validation — the battery, and nothing anchored on a prior instrument

Five anchors under oracle/fixtures/cell-anchors, each cell a shell-derived fact
(scripts/cell-anchors.mjs re-derives them by execution; run receipts in the suite). Two
fresh blind readers per anchor over a path-neutral copy WITH TRUTH.json STRIPPED — the
declared cell never reaches a reader.

| --- | --- |

## Pass criterion, pre-stated

10/10 readings match the shell-derived cells. Any miss and v3 does not ship, exactly as
v2 did not.

## What passing would and would not establish

Passing validates v3 on the DECIDABLE CORE only — emissions whose cell is a shell fact.
The mixed-mode shapes (a salvage note wrapped around a routing sheet; a recipe-less guide
in finished voice) have no shell-decidable cell, and v3's readings of them would be new
instrument output, not validated truth. Shipping means: the procedure doc gains v3 as the
classification question for FUTURE grounding, its own cohort; existing rows keep their v1
classifications and pinned hashes, and any re-grounding is separately priced work.
