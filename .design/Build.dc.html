THE FAILURE THIS INTRODUCES
If one critic slot dissents systematically rather than noisily, unanimity never converges. With no ratchet and no round cap it burns budget while the builder keeps fixing a gap that may not be the real problem.

It does not fail loudly — it fails by never stopping. Escalation limits the cost to one critic per round, which makes it cheap enough to notice.
