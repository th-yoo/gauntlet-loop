The ratchet drop has a counter-anchor
The canonical iterative-refinement paper caps at four iterations and selects the best output across all of them, precisely because quality fluctuates. Our drop rests on the architecture — a Workflow script cannot hold a prior version — not on evidence that regressions do not happen.
