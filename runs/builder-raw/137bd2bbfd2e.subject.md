# Denby Warehouse Stock Tracking System — Procurement Action Plan

**Status: system not yet in place.** This document tracks the process from
RFP publication through to a working system in use by staff. It is a plan
and tracker, not the system itself.

## Source

Derived from `RFP.md` (Request for proposals — warehouse inventory system).

## Key dates and facts, as stated in the RFP

- Submission deadline: 14 November, to procurement@denby-coop.example
- Award notified within three weeks of the closing date (by approximately
  5 December)
- A single supplier is awarded
- The evaluation panel meets once, after the closing date
- The cooperative does not build the system itself — the awarded supplier
  delivers it
- Evaluation weighting: 40% cost, 40% approach, 20% support terms

## What a compliant proposal must cover, per the RFP

- Approach to stock counts across three storage bays
- Barcode hardware to be supplied, and its cost
- Training for six warehouse staff
- Support terms for the first twelve months

## Steps remaining to reach a working system in use by staff

1. Publish/circulate the RFP to prospective suppliers (if not already done),
   ahead of 14 November
2. Receive and log proposals by the 14 November deadline
3. Convene the evaluation panel after closing; score each proposal against
   the 40% cost / 40% approach / 20% support weighting
4. Notify the awarded supplier within three weeks of closing
5. Awarded supplier delivers barcode hardware and the stock-count approach
   across the three bays
6. Awarded supplier trains the six warehouse staff
7. Twelve-month support term begins
8. Confirm staff are actively using the system day-to-day — this is the
   actual completion criterion, not the award decision

## What cannot be resolved from this document alone

- No proposal has been submitted, so no scoring, award, hardware delivery,
  or training can happen yet
- No details of current stock-tracking practice, warehouse layout, or IT
  environment are given — a supplier will need these, but they are not
  addressed here
- No supplier, product, or cost has been chosen; none is fabricated here

## Status against the goal

Goal: "the Denby warehouse has a working stock tracking system in use by
staff."

**Not met.** The RFP is the opening step of a multi-week procurement
process (submission → evaluation → award → delivery → training → support).
No system exists, and none can exist, until a supplier is awarded and
completes delivery and training — none of which has occurred yet.
