# Contract front-end developer — marketing site (6 weeks)

**Cadence** is a small scheduling product. We are hiring one contract front-end
developer to build the launch landing page for it. This is a fixed-scope
engagement, remote, starting the first week of next month.

## The engagement

You will build and ship one marketing landing page for Cadence. Our marketing
lead will give you the approved copy, the brand files and the deadline at kickoff
— none of that is settled yet and none of it is your call to make.

Six weeks, part time. Day rate depending on experience.

## What we are looking for

- Hand-written HTML and CSS. We are not looking for a framework rebuild.
- Someone who has shipped marketing pages that score well on Core Web Vitals.
- Comfortable working from a brief without a designer sitting beside you.
- Available for a weekly check-in with the marketing lead.

## Nice to have

- SVG illustration, or a working relationship with an illustrator.
- Experience with a static host and a CDN.

## How to apply

Send a short note and two links to work you have shipped to
`careers@cadence.example`, subject line `LANDING PAGE CONTRACT`. Include your day
rate and your earliest start date.

Applications close on the 30th. We will reply to everyone who writes in, and we
will run one 45-minute call with a shortlist before we decide.

*Cadence is an equal opportunity employer.*
