# Before anything gets built: the competitive teardown

Nothing goes to the studio until this is done. Your job here is the teardown, not
the page — the studio designs the page from what you write, in their next sprint,
and they have been told to wait for it.

## What to produce

A single document, `teardown.md`, covering **three** rival scheduling products'
landing pages. For each one:

- the headline and subhead, quoted exactly as they appear;
- what the primary call to action asks the visitor to do, and where it sits;
- how the page handles the objection "why not just email to arrange it";
- one thing it does that we should steal, and one it does that we should avoid.

Close with half a page: the three patterns common to all of them, and the one
position none of them has taken, which is where Cadence should sit.

## How to work

Pick the three yourself — anything a small team would plausibly compare us
against. Read the pages themselves rather than reviews of them. Where a page is
unreachable, say so and pick another, and say which you replaced.

Quote rather than paraphrase. The studio will argue with your conclusions and
they cannot do that with a summary.

## When it is done

Send `teardown.md` to the marketing lead, who forwards it to the studio with the
brief. Do not send it to the studio directly and do not start on layout,
wireframes or copy for our own page — that work is theirs, and duplicating it
here has caused rework before.
