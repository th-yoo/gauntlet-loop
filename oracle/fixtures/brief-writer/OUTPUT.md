# Creative brief: Cadence landing page

For: [External design studio]
From: [Account manager]

## Product in one sentence

Cadence is a scheduling product that lets people share their availability and get booked directly, without the back-and-forth of finding a shared time.

## Who it is for

Independent consultants, small business owners, and team leads who currently coordinate meetings by email or text and want a faster, more professional booking experience for the people they meet with.

## What the page must make a visitor do

Start a free Cadence account (primary call to action). A secondary path should let visitors preview a live sample booking page before signing up.

## Tone, and three references we like

Tone: clear and confident, low-friction, plain language over corporate jargon, generous whitespace.

References:
- Calendly's homepage — simplicity of the booking-flow demo front and center
- Linear's marketing site — typography, pacing, restraint
- Superhuman's landing page — one strong value proposition above the fold

## What is out of scope

No pricing page, no integrations marketplace, no blog or content section this round. No illustration or custom iconography; if imagery is used, avoid stock photos of people.

## Deadline the studio should work to

First-round concepts due two weeks from brief handoff. Questions before then should go to the account manager, not engineering.
