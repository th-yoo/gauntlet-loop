# Request for proposals — warehouse inventory system

The cooperative invites proposals from suppliers to design and deliver a stock
tracking system for the Denby warehouse.

**Submit by:** 14 November, to procurement@denby-coop.example
**Award:** a single supplier, notified within three weeks of the closing date

## What a proposal must cover

- Approach to stock counts across three storage bays
- Barcode hardware you would supply, and its cost
- Training for six warehouse staff
- Support terms for the first twelve months

## How proposals are evaluated

Weighted: 40% cost, 40% approach, 20% support terms. The evaluation panel meets
once after the closing date. We do not build anything ourselves; the awarded
supplier delivers the system.
